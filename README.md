### About

I am a full stack developer and Software Engineering student at **SMK Telkom Purwokerto**, writing code since **2019**. I am also the founder of **Papercode**, a digital services agency that builds websites, POS systems, and business applications for SMEs and companies across Indonesia.

My work focuses on turning real business problems into reliable, scalable software, from backend services and APIs to web dashboards and mobile apps.

### Focus Areas

- **Backend Engineering**: REST APIs and services with Go, Laravel, and Python
- **Web Development**: modern frontends with Next.js, React, and Tailwind CSS
- **Mobile Development**: cross-platform apps with React Native (Expo) and Flutter
- **Business Systems**: POS, inventory, and operational tools for the F&B and retail sector
- **Cloud and Infrastructure**: deployment, Linux servers, Nginx, and Docker

### Currently

- Building products and client solutions under **Papercode**
- Exploring system design and scalable architecture for multi-outlet businesses
- Open to collaboration on web, mobile, and backend projects
